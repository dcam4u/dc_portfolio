About Me

Building the Future, One Line at a Time

I'm a front-end web developer based in Phoenix, Arizona, and an aspiring software engineer with over 20 years of experience in tech and customer-facing roles. After two decades of solving problems and connecting with people, I made the decision to pivot into a career that truly combines my creative passion with my love for building solutions.

Currently, I'm furthering my education in software engineering while building a strong foundation in HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and frameworks like React. I'm also exploring backend technologies as I work toward becoming a full-stack developer.

My goal is to create responsive, accessible, and beautiful digital experiences that help users feel connected and empowered. When I'm not coding, you'll find me exploring design trends, diving deeper into JavaScript, or curating collectibles. Whether I'm studying, building projects, or parenting my daughter, I show up every day with grit, curiosity, and a clear vision for the future.
